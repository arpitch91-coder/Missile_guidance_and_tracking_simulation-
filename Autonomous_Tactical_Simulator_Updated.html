MATHEMATICAL FOUNDATIONS

Guidance Laws & Kinematics Theory

An intuitive and mathematical breakdown of proportional navigation, drag equations, and seeker noise mitigation.

1. Proportional Navigation Guidance (PNG)

Proportional Navigation is the universal guidance law used by modern homing missiles. Instead of heading directly at the target's current location (pure pursuit), PNG commands a lateral acceleration proportional to the rotational rate of the Line-of-Sight (LOS) angle.

ac = N · Vc · λ̇
N (Nav Constant): Typically tuned between 3.0 and 5.0 to ensure intercept convergence before fuel depletion.
Vc (Closing Velocity): The rate at which relative separation distance between missile and target is collapsing.
λ̇ (LOS Rate): The angular rotation velocity of the line-of-sight vector measured by the onboard radar seeker.

2. Atmospheric Drag & Thrust Mass Decay

Flight kinematics account for fluid drag forces and variable mass during motor burn. Air density drops exponentially with altitude according to the scale height model:

AERODYNAMIC DRAG FORCE
Fd = ½ · ρ(y) · v² · Cd · A
ATMOSPHERIC DENSITY DECAY
ρ(y) = ρ₀ · e(-y / 8500)

3. Seeker Radar Jitter & Low-Pass Filtering

Real radar seekers experience target glint and electronic noise. Unfiltered noise causes extreme derivative spikes in the LOS rate (λ̇), inducing violent steering commands that bleed kinetic energy. We apply exponential low-pass smoothing:

λ̇filtered = (α · λ̇raw) + (1 - α) · λ̇previous
